# AI Robotics — Research Brief

Research date: 2026-08-16. Source: cenobots.com, reseller listings, competitor sites.
Purpose: ground the design/build of the AI Robotics marketing site.

---

## 1. What Cenobots actually sells

Commercial and industrial **autonomous floor-cleaning robots**, sold B2B into facilities.
Not consumer. Not humanoids. The buyer is a facility manager, EVS manager, COO, or
procurement lead.

Positioning line on their own site: *"Best Commercial & Autonomous Cleaning Robots."*
Primary CTA everywhere: *"Schedule a free demo with us."*

**Industry verticals they target:** Smart Retail, Smart Transportation, Smart Education,
Smart Healthcare, Smart Hospitality. Plus warehouses/logistics/manufacturing for the S5.

## 3. Proof and ROI ammunition

These are the numbers that close B2B deals. Cenobots buries them; we should lead with them.

- **~$0.008 per sq ft** — roughly 5–7× cheaper than human labor.
- **~50% labor cost reduction**, ~30% supply savings.
- **345+ autonomous hours/month** on a deployed L50 — the equivalent of two full-time
  staff, at a fraction of the cost.
- **9–12 month payback** on large open floor plans.
- **~62 dB** operation — quiet enough to run in occupied retail during business hours.
- Consistency: floor mapping gives identical coverage every night, removing the
  variability of staff turnover and fatigue.

## 4. Their site, audited

Built on Next.js — same framework we're using. Structure:

`About Us · Products · Solutions · Technologies · Cases & Testimonials · News · Blog · Partner Center · Contact`

Product page section order (consistent across all 5 robots):
hero → spec overview → intelligent cleaning → mobile app → safety → maintenance →
sustainability → accessories → dimensions table → usage scenarios → demo CTA → footer.

### Extracted design tokens

| Token | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary blue | `#1257FA` | most-used brand color |
| Blue variant | `#0B55F8` | near-duplicate, inconsistent |
| Near-black | `#0B0814` / `#1A1A1A` | |
| Greys | `#888` `#CCC` `#D9D9D9` `#EDEDED` `#FAFAFA` | |
| Alert red | `#E72410` | |
| Radii | `0`, `10px`, `25px`, `50%`, pill halves | inconsistent |
| Shadow | `0 15px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.1)` | one heavy, dated shadow |
| **Font** | **system stack + SimSun fallback** | **no brand typeface at all** |

### Where they are weak — our openings

1. **No typography identity.** They ship the default OS font stack with a `SimSun`
   fallback. A real typeface alone will make us look like a more serious company.
2. **Inconsistent design system.** Two near-identical blues, five unrelated corner radii,
   one dated drop shadow. No token discipline.
3. **Specs are buried.** The numbers that actually sell — coverage rate, payback period,
   passage width — sit at the bottom of pages behind generic feature copy.
4. **No ROI calculator.** Every ROI claim is prose in a blog post. A facility manager
   cannot enter their square footage and see a payback number. This is the single
   biggest conversion gap in the category.
5. **Thin CTA.** "Schedule a free demo" with no supporting qualification. No lead capture
   depth, no financing/lease messaging on-page despite monthly pricing existing.
6. **No real comparison tool.** Third-party resellers built the L3-vs-L4-vs-L50 comparison
   pages that rank — the manufacturer didn't. Buyers genuinely need this.
7. **Marketing writes for the engineer, not the CFO.** Category-wide failure per industry
   research: engineering content earns the shortlist, but pilots die at the CFO desk.

## 6. Design direction options

### Option A — Industrial Graphite + Safety Amber *(recommended)*
Deep graphite/near-black base, warm amber-orange accent, clean white surfaces.
- **Why:** amber reads as *industrial safety* (hi-vis vests, warning beacons, heavy
  equipment) — it is native to the warehouse and facilities world, not borrowed from
  consumer tech. Maximum contrast against a blue category. Photographs of white/grey
  robots pop against graphite. Warm accent feels human and approachable, which softens
  the "robots replacing jobs" objection.
- **Risk:** amber must be handled carefully so it doesn't read as a warning/error state.
  Reserve red strictly for genuine errors.

### Option B — Deep Space + Precision Cyan
Near-black `#0B0B10` base, cool cyan/electric accent, spacecraft-instrument feel.
- **Why:** leans into the AI/LiDAR/perception story. Dark mode as default is a 2026 B2B
  trend and makes robot product photography look premium.
- **Risk:** cyan is still adjacent to blue; less differentiation than A. Dark-only sites
  can hurt readability for spec-heavy tables that facility managers actually read.

### Option C — Clinical White + Signal Lime
Near-white, generous whitespace, single high-voltage lime accent.
- **Why:** hospitals and healthcare are a major vertical; clinical cleanliness *is* the
  product. Whitespace signals premium.
- **Risk:** closest to Avidbots' green. Bright/airy can read as less "industrial-tough"
  for the warehouse buyer.

### Typography recommendation
Give us the brand voice Cenobots entirely lacks.
- **Headings:** Space Grotesk — distinctive, technical, slightly mechanical character.
- **Body:** DM Sans or Inter — highly readable for dense spec content.
- **Specs/data:** JetBrains Mono — monospace numerals make spec tables and the ROI
  calculator feel like instrumentation readouts rather than marketing.

That heading/body pairing is catalogued in the skill DB as "Tech Startup" — tech,
innovative, futuristic — and fits an AI robotics brand precisely.

2. Key stats band — coverage rate, tank, runtime, passage width
3. Signature feature — the one thing only this robot does
4. Intelligence — AI compute, LiDAR, perception, obstacle avoidance
5. Safety
6. App & fleet management
7. Maintenance & serviceability
8. Accessories / workstation
9. Full spec table (with unit toggle — metric/imperial)
10. Best-fit environments
11. Cross-sell: compare with other models

### Contact / quote form — required by your brief
Fields: **Name\*, Email\*, Phone\*, Company, Address, Interested robot** (dropdown,
pre-filled when arriving from a product page), Facility size, Message.

Recommended additions: honeypot + rate limiting for spam, inline validation, a success
state that sets expectations ("we respond within one business day"), and a server action
that both persists the lead and emails the sales inbox.

## 8. Differentiators to build that Cenobots does not have

1. **ROI / payback calculator** — enter facility sq ft, cleaning hours, labor rate; get
   monthly savings and payback period. Biggest gap in the category.
2. **Robot finder / selector** — 3 questions (space type, floor area, wet or dry debris)
   → recommended model. Turns 5 confusing SKUs into one clear answer.
3. **Real comparison table** — all five models side by side, filterable.
4. **Unit toggle** — metric/imperial throughout. The lineup spans both markets.
5. **Quote form pre-filled by context** — arriving from `/robots/l4` pre-selects the L4.

## 10. Decisions made (2026-08-16)

### ✅ DECIDED — Color direction: Industrial Graphite + Safety Amber
Deep graphite base, warm amber-orange accent, white surfaces. Chosen specifically to
break out of the blue category. Amber is native to industrial safety (hi-vis, warning
beacons, heavy equipment) rather than borrowed from consumer tech.

Starting token set (to be refined against WCAG during the build):

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `base` | `#12141A` | near-black graphite ground |
| `surface` | `#1C1F26` | raised panels, cards |
| `accent` | `#FF9A1F` | safety amber — primary CTA, key stats |
| `accent-hot` | `#FFB84D` | hover / active |
| `paper` | `#FAFAF8` | light sections |
| `ink` | `#0A0B0E` | text on light |

Constraint: reserve red strictly for genuine error states so amber never reads as a
warning. Verify amber-on-graphite and any amber-on-white text hits 4.5:1 — amber
frequently fails on white, so use it for fills and large display type there, not body text.

### ✅ DECIDED — Brand relationship: Authorized distributor / reseller
AI Robotics sells Cenobots robots under its own storefront. Implications:
- Keep Cenobots model names: **L3, L4, L50, SP50, S5**.
- Display "Authorized Cenobots Partner" trust signaling.
- Look **visually independent** from cenobots.com (hence: no blue) while borrowing their
  engineering credibility and the NVIDIA/LiDAR technology story.
- Testimonials must be attributed as Cenobots deployments, not AI Robotics customers.

### ✅ DECIDED — Pricing: no prices, quote only
No sticker prices or monthly figures on the site. Every CTA drives to the quote form.
Reseller prices in §2 are for internal tier positioning only — **do not publish**.
Implication: the ROI calculator outputs savings/payback framing rather than a price,
and ends in a quote request.

### Still open
1. **Territory / market?** Drives default units (metric vs imperial), currency, language,
   and phone number format. Build the unit toggle regardless.
2. **Can we use those testimonials?** They are Cenobots' customers. Attribute clearly
   until confirmed.
3. **Where do form submissions go?** Email inbox, CRM, or database — needed before the
   contact page ships. Default to a server action that persists plus emails.
4. **Robot photography** — user to supply. Drop into `public/robots/<model>/`.
